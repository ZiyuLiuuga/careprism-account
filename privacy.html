CarePrism Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-05 · Test version for invited testers

CarePrism helps people with long-term conditions and their caregivers keep track of readings, medicines, symptoms, appointments and medical records. This page explains what the app stores, who can see it and what you can do about it.

What we store

  • Your account: your e-mail address and a password (stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider).
  • What you enter: profiles of the people you look after, conditions, readings (for example blood pressure, glucose, weight), medicines and doses taken, diary entries, appointments, reminders and medical records you add.
  • Apple Health (iPhone, only if you turn it on): the readings you allow, such as heart rate, blood pressure, blood oxygen, sleep and steps. We only read; we never write to Apple Health.

How it is used

  • To show your records, charts, reminders and alerts in the app.
  • The assistant (AI): when you ask a question or use a feature that reads a photo or document, the relevant information is sent to Anthropic's Claude service to produce the answer. Under Settings → Assistant privacy you choose what is held back. In photos and PDF pages the items you list (names, address, phone numbers, ID numbers and anything you add) are covered on your phone before anything is sent, and you see the covered picture first.
  • We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use Apple Health data for any purpose other than showing it to you and the people you share with.

Who can see it

  • You, and the people in a person's care circle that you (or they) add. Access is enforced in the database: one account cannot read another person's records unless it has been linked to them.
  • The service providers that run the app on our behalf: Supabase (database and sign-in, servers in the United States) and Anthropic (the assistant, only for the requests you make).

Keeping it safe

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Each request is checked against your sign-in. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong password and do not share your account.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete readings, entries and records in the app at any time.
  • Export a person's complete file (Settings → Patient files → Export).
  • Turn off Apple Health access in the iPhone Settings app (Health → Data Access & Devices → CarePrism).
  • To delete your account and all records you own, contact the person who invited you to test CarePrism.

Not for emergencies

CarePrism is not a medical device and does not give a diagnosis. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Changes

If this policy changes, the app asks you to read and accept the new version.

CarePrism 隐私政策

版本 2026-11-05 · 受邀测试人员的测试版

CarePrism 帮助慢性病患者和照护者记录读数、用药、症状、预约和病历。本页说明 app 保存哪些信息、谁能看到，以及你可以做什么。

我们保存什么

  • 账号：邮箱和密码（登录服务只以安全哈希形式保存密码）。
  • 你录入的内容：你照护的人的资料、病情、读数（如血压、血糖、体重）、药物和服药记录、日记、预约、提醒和你添加的病历。
  • Apple 健康（仅 iPhone，且仅在你打开时）：你允许读取的数据，如心率、血压、血氧、睡眠和步数。我们只读取，从不写入 Apple 健康。

如何使用

  • 在 app 里显示你的记录、图表、提醒和警报。
  • AI 助手：当你提问、或使用读取照片和文件的功能时，相关信息会发送给 Anthropic 的 Claude 服务来生成回答。你可以在 设置 → AI 隐私 中选择哪些内容不发送。照片和 PDF 中你列出的内容（姓名、地址、电话、编号及你自己添加的）会先在你的手机上遮盖，你确认遮盖后的图片后才会发送。
  • 我们不出售你的数据，不用于广告，Apple 健康数据只用于显示给你和你共享的人。

谁能看到

  • 你本人，以及你（或对方）添加到某人照护圈里的人。访问权限由数据库强制执行：一个账号无法读取未与其关联的人的记录。
  • 代我们运行 app 的服务商：Supabase（数据库和登录，服务器位于美国）和 Anthropic（AI 助手，仅处理你发起的请求）。

安全

数据在传输中加密（HTTPS），每个请求都会校验你的登录。没有系统是绝对安全的，请使用强密码，不要共享账号。

你的选择

  • 随时在 app 中修改或删除读数、记录和病历。
  • 导出某人的完整档案（设置 → 病人档案 → 导出）。
  • 在 iPhone 设置中关闭 Apple 健康访问（健康 → 数据访问与设备 → CarePrism）。
  • 如需删除账号及你拥有的全部记录，请联系邀请你测试 CarePrism 的人。

不用于紧急情况

CarePrism 不是医疗器械，不提供诊断。紧急情况请拨打当地急救电话。

变更

如果本政策有变化，app 会请你阅读并同意新版本。
